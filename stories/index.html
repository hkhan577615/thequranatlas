How it works

Pick a book and open its brief. The brief lists every page: the words that go on it, the picture there now, and any hidden detail the picture must keep, because the “Look closer” page at the back of each book points readers to it.

  • Do not draw any prophet. In this series no person is drawn at all: tell the story through places, light, animals and objects.
  • Keep each hidden detail, such as the 950 stars in Nuh or the bowl shaped like ن in Yunus.
  • Square pages, 8.5 × 8.5 in. Leave the top or bottom third calm enough for the words.
  • Your pictures are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the rest of the series, and you are credited on the title page of every copy.

Open, from start to finish

  • The books

    Words and pictures under CC BY-SA 4.0. Copy them, print them, translate them, or redraw them, and share what you make the same way.

  • The verses

    Arabic text from the Tanzil Project, quoted exactly. Counts and hidden details are computed with the same open data as the Quran Atlas.

  • The code

    The scripts that write and draw every page are open source, so anyone can build a new edition, or a new language.

Each story is checked against the verses it retells; the notes for parents at the back of every book list them. A scholar’s review is still to come, and corrections are welcome.
