Illustrators wanted

These first pictures are a starting point. Anyone can redraw a book, in any style, and the best versions will replace them, with full credit.

How it works

Pick a book and open its brief. The brief lists every page: the words that go on it, the picture there now, and any hidden detail the picture must keep, because the “Look closer” page at the back of each book points readers to it.

  • Do not draw any prophet. In this series no person is drawn at all: tell the story through places, light, animals and objects.
  • Keep each hidden detail, such as the 950 stars in Nuh or the bowl shaped like ن in Yunus.
  • Square pages, 8.5 × 8.5 in. Leave the top or bottom third calm enough for the words.
  • You keep the copyright in your pictures. By sending them, you let Quran Atlas use them in the books, online and in print, and share them under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 like the rest of the series. You are credited on the title page of every copy.
